RamaKoti – Privacy Policy

For the RamaKoti app on Google Play

Publisher: SHAYAN TOPLINE COMPUTER'S EDUCATION PRIVATE LIMITED

App: RamaKoti (Android, package com.ramakoti.app)

Last updated: 1 November 2026

RamaKoti ("the App") is published on Google Play by SHAYAN TOPLINE COMPUTER'S EDUCATION PRIVATE LIMITED ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the App handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or store any personal information. The App has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no servers of its own, and it does not send your data to us.

Information stored on your device

The following information is stored only in the App's private storage on your device:

  • your Rama Nama counts and daily history;
  • your milestones, journey progress and the blessings you have seen;
  • your settings (such as language, theme, mantra and vibration);
  • your free-trial status and whether you have purchased Rama Koti Lifetime;
  • the optional name you enter for your completion certificate.

We cannot access this information. It stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Android backup

If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the App's data in your device backup in your Google account, and may restore it when you set up a new device or reinstall the App. That backup is managed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, and we cannot access it. You can manage or delete your backups in your device settings or your Google account.

In-app purchase

The App offers an optional one-time purchase, Rama Koti Lifetime, which unlocks unlimited chanting after the first 108 free Rama Nama. It is not a subscription.

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment card or billing details. The App uses Google Play Billing, a Google library, to start the purchase and to learn from Google Play whether you own it, so that it can unlock unlimited chanting. Information processed by Google Play and Google Play Billing is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Billing – to make the in-app purchase through Google Play.
  • Internet and network state – included by the Google Play Billing library and used for communicating with Google Play. The App itself does not connect to any other server.
  • Vibrate – for an optional short vibration on each Japa Mala bead, which you can turn off in the App.

The App does not request access to your microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos or files.

Deleting your data

Because your information is stored on your device, you control it. Clearing the App's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the App, permanently deletes the App's data from your device. A copy may remain in your Android device backup until you delete that backup in your device or Google account settings. Your purchase of Rama Koti Lifetime is recorded by Google Play, not by us, and can be restored from Google Play when you reinstall the App with the same Google account.

Children

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at business@shayantoplinecomputers.com.
